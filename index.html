About me

  
BIM technician specialising in modelling, coordination and information quality

  

   
I am a BIM technician based in the United Kingdom, working at Mejarc Integrated Services since 2024. Before that I drew for a residential developer at Wren Living, spent three years as an architectural technologist at Barkwaj Consult, and started out as a design technician with an army engineers unit. I hold an MSc in Project Management for construction and the built environment, and a B.Tech in Architecture.

   
Most portfolios show finished buildings. This one shows the state the information was in, and what I changed so that a team could use it. Clash counts mean nothing until each clash has an owner, a warning count means nothing until the leftovers have reasons, and a valid IFC means nothing until someone opens it and checks the data.

   
I work in Revit 2027 and Navisworks Manage, publish through Autodesk Construction Cloud, and script the repetitive parts so every change is logged and reversible.

  

  
A warning count of zero usually means someone deleted something that mattered. I would rather show you the seven I kept and why.
